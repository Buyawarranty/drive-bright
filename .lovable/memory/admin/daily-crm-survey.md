---
name: Daily CRM survey rules
description: Daily agent CRM feedback survey — launch date 2026-09-09, daily silent pop-up 16:30–17:30 UK, agents see only their own answers, managers see all + per-agent problem charts
type: feature
---
- Survey went live 2026-09-09 (`SURVEY_START_DATE`). Never show or expect days before it in completion grids or charts.
- Agents (sales, sales_lead) get a silent pop-up "Please fill in your CRM feedback survey" every day once the 16:30 UK window opens, on any dashboard tab, with a link to their CRM feedback page. Dismiss = hidden for that day only; gone once sent. No sound.
- Agents can open the Staff System Reports tab but only ever see their OWN questions/answers and chart. Managers/admin/super_admin see everyone, plus a "Problems by agent" chart per agent.
- Questions are fixed: Orders page issues, Orders "Other", New Leads issues, New Leads "Other", 1–5 rating, biggest issue, anything else. One answer per agent per day.
