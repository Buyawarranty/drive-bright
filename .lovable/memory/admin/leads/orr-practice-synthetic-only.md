---
Management chose "practice mode, made-up leads only" for Open Round Robin on 29 October 2026.

- The rehearsal runs on invented customers only, using phone numbers Ofcom reserves for fiction and example.com addresses, so a practice lead can never be dialled by accident.
- It must not be able to reach a real customer, a real lead, a text, a WhatsApp message or the phone system.
- Website lead triggers are rehearsed with simulated journey events. Production website tracking is not changed without separate approval.
- Outbound calls are never connected from the sandbox.
