---
name: ORR timers and chase cadence
description: Open Round Robin rhythm agreed 29 Oct 2026 — two-minute quiet release, three-minute claim hold, two-hourly round-the-clock re-release with no cap, ownership only from a confirmed conversation
type: feature
---
**Release clock**
- An identifiable customer who goes quiet for two minutes on the pricing journey, or two minutes in checkout, is released into the Open Round Robin pool. Both thresholds are manager-editable.
- Anonymous browsers are never turned into telephone leads.
- A customer who asks for a call-back waits until the time they asked for.
- A customer who comes back has proactive calling paused again.

**Claim hold**
- An agent who takes a lead holds it exclusively for three minutes (manager-editable).
- If nobody starts work, it returns to the pool straight away rather than waiting for the next review.

**Ownership**
- A claim is a temporary exclusive hold, never ownership.
- Only a confirmed conversation with the customer makes the lead theirs, confirmed through Zoiper and Dial Nine.
- Voicemails are disregarded for ownership however long they ran.

**Chase and re-release**
- Round the clock with no attempt cap: an unworked lead is reviewed every two hours (manager-selectable 30, 60, 120, 240 or 480 minutes) until it is genuinely worked, converted, appropriately closed or suppressed.
- An internal re-release must never place a call by itself.
- Permitted calling hours stay separate from the round-the-clock intake clock: Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday 12pm to 4pm.
- A confirmed purchase, an opt-out or a complaint stops outreach.

**Earlier rhythm, superseded on 29 Oct 2026**
- Two-minute browsing window with a two-minute reservation, and twice-daily chasing at 09:30 and 15:30 for seven days.
