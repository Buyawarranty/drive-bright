---
name: Miles live agent and contact options
description: Human chat is duty-toggle controlled; otherwise Miles offers Call us, WhatsApp us and Request a callback
type: feature
---
Miles may offer a live human chat only when a staff member has explicitly switched **On duty** in the Miles admin area and their heartbeat is fresh.

Never say a specialist is joining, being connected, alerted, or will arrive "in a moment" before a human has actually replied.

When no specialist is on duty, the customer contact card must show exactly these three actions:
1. Call us
2. WhatsApp us
3. Request a callback

Do not show Request an email or Request a WhatsApp as separate actions in that unavailable-agent card.
