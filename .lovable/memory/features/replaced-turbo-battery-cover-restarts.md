---
name: Replaced turbo / hybrid battery cover restarts
description: Replaced turbo unit or hybrid/EV drive battery stays covered with receipt; the 7yr/80k turbo and 10yr/80k battery clock restarts from the replacement date
type: feature
---
A used or older vehicle's turbo unit, and a hybrid/EV drive battery, ARE covered if they have been replaced, provided a valid receipt and proof of purchase for the replacement part is supplied.

- Turbo limit (7 years / 80,000 miles) and drive battery limit (10 years / 80,000 miles) are measured from the replacement part, not the vehicle's original registration date or lifetime mileage.
- The duration of cover for that component starts again from the date the replacement was fitted.
- Never state an older turbo or hybrid battery is outside cover without first asking whether it has been replaced.
